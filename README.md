Egg Repack Tally Tracker — How It Works

What is this app for?
This app helps egg-packing workers keep count of how many eggs they repack (put into new cartons) and how many are damaged (broken or missing) during a work session. At the end, it gives you a summary showing totals in both                 individual eggs and full cartons.

Getting Started — Sessions
When you open the app, you're asked to either start a new session or resume your last session. A session is one stretch of counting work — think of it like clocking in and out. Everything you count belongs to that session.

Choosing an Egg Type
You pick from 5 egg types:
Extra Large White (comes in cartons of 12)
Large White (cartons of 12)
Large Brown (cartons of 12)
Medium White (cartons of 12)
Large White Flat (comes in flats of 30)

Entering a Count
For each carton you inspect, you tell the app how many eggs are damaged (or missing). The app then figures out how many good eggs were repacked using simple subtraction:
Eggs Repacked = Carton Size − (Damaged + Missing)
For example, if you're checking a carton of 12 Large White eggs and 3 are damaged:
Repacked: 12 − 3 = 9 eggs saved
Damaged: 3 eggs lost
For a flat of 30 Large White Flat eggs with 5 damaged:
Repacked: 30 − 5 = 25 eggs saved
Damaged: 5 eggs lost
Special Rule: Large White + Large White Flat
The app combines the repacked counts from Large White (12-packs) and Large White Flat (30-packs) into one combined total. This is because repacked eggs from flats go into the same pile as regular Large White eggs. However, the damaged counts are tracked separately since the carton sizes are different.

Running Tallies
As you work, the app keeps a running total of repacked and damaged eggs for each type. You can see where you stand at any time.

Fixing Mistakes
Undo: If you enter something wrong, you can undo your last entry to remove it.
Manual Adjustment: You can also manually bump the repacked or damaged tally up or down for any egg type — handy if something was miscounted earlier.

Leftovers
At the end of a session, you can record leftover eggs — partial cartons or loose eggs that don't make up a full count. These get added to the tallies as adjustments so your final numbers are accurate.

Finishing Up — The Summary
When you're done counting, you press "Counting Complete" and the app shows a final summary with:
Repacked (eggs)
Repacked (cartons)
Damaged (eggs)
Damaged (cartons)

Carton counts are calculated by dividing the total eggs by the carton size and rounding down to whole cartons (no fractions):
Cartons = Total Eggs ÷ Carton Size (rounded down)
So 25 repacked eggs ÷ 12 per carton = 2 cartons (with 1 egg left over, not counted as a carton).
